# Discriminator review

A registry candidate records driver and probe scope, decoded response region,
normalization, supporting bundles/devices, contradictions, observed values,
physical-declaration correlations, counts, confidence scope and review history.

Automatic statuses are limited to `candidate`, `insufficient-evidence`,
`correlated`, and `contradicted`. `verified-electronic-identifier` is rejected by
schema validation in M1.3. Correlation cannot change hardware identity or
firmware compatibility.

A reviewer must establish independent physical ground truth, compatible driver
and command scope, repeatability within devices, separation between hardware
groups, absence or explanation of contradictions, and documented limitations.
Catalog proposals are review artifacts, never catalog mutations.
