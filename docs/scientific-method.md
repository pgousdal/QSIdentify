# Scientific Method

QSIdentify reports confidence as independent dimensions: transport, protocol,
firmware, fingerprint, statistical, review and catalog confidence. These
dimensions are never averaged or collapsed into a single confidence value.

Project thresholds are policy gates, not scientific proof:

- one independent radio supports stability analysis;
- two support comparison;
- three permit candidate correlation;
- five independently inspected radios permit human review;
- ten independently verified radios permit a catalog proposal.

Repeated captures from one physical radio are not independent devices.
Statistical correlation can identify a useful discriminator candidate, but it
cannot establish a hardware identity by itself. Physical inspection, electronic
observations and reviewer conclusions remain visibly separate. Unknown or
contradictory responses are preserved as evidence rather than forced into a
catalog mapping.
