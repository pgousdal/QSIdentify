# Evidence policy

QSIdentify distinguishes electronically observed bytes, user-declared physical
facts, curated catalog metadata, descriptive statistics, inferred candidates,
and confirmed identity. These categories are never silently promoted.

One pseudonymous device is useful for stability only. Two devices permit a
preliminary comparison. Three or more devices may establish a correlation.
Hardware mapping additionally requires multiple independently inspected groups.
Any contradiction blocks a proposal. These thresholds are project policy, not
scientific proof; repeated captures from one radio do not increase device count.

Withdrawn declarations are excluded. Conflicting MCU or PCB declarations,
fingerprints associated with contradictory inspected hardware, incompatible
drivers/commands, impossible chronology and multi-device bundle claims receive
stable reason codes. Missing values remain unknown.

Privacy is structural: registries reject forbidden host keys and home paths;
contributions exclude raw captures, host identity, USB serials, firmware,
executables and unsafe archive paths. All workflows are offline.

> A repeated fingerprint is evidence of repeated electronic output, not proof
> of identical MCU, PCB revision or marketed model.
